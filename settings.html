Bangla order message

The message you copy and send to customers (Sales → ⋯ → বাংলা সামারি). Placeholders in curly brackets are filled in automatically.

Placeholders

{name} {phone} {address} {orderNo} {invoiceNo} {date} {items} {subtotal} {discount} {area} {delivery} {total} {advance} {due} {deliveryTime} {payment} {notes} {business} {contact}

A line with {discount}, {advance}, {deliveryTime}, {notes} or {contact} is left out when that value is empty. {items} becomes one line per product.
