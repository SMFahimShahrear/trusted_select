Placeholders you can use in your own Word files

Order

{invoiceNo} {orderNo} {date} {dateDots} {status} {payment} {paymentStatus} {trxId} {notes} {estDelivery} {printedOn} {businessName}

Customer

{customerName} {customerPhone} {customerAddress} {customerEmail} {deliveryZone}

Money

{subtotal} {discount} {deliveryCharge} {total} {advancePaid} {due}

"Tk 1,830". Add Amount for "1,830" or Fixed for "1,830.00", e.g. {totalFixed}. {due} = cash the courier collects.

Items

In a table row: {#items} in the first cell, {/items} in the last. Use {#rows}…{/rows} instead to keep 6 rows with empty ones.

{no} {name} {code} {qty} {qty2} {price} {priceFixed} {lineTotal} {lineTotalFixed}

Tick boxes (☒ / ☐)

{codBox} {bankBox} {mfsBox} {cashBox} {paidBox} {partBox} {unpaidBox}

2 per page

Put {#inv1}…{/inv1} around the left invoice and {#inv2}…{/inv2} around the right one.

Type each placeholder in one go with both curly brackets. Several orders print on separate pages automatically.
